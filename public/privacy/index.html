Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026

This policy explains how NoboSole handles information when wholesale customers use the NoboSole Android app and web storefront.

Information we process

  • Google account identity, including name, email address and profile image.
  • Business profile details such as phone number, shop or company name, trade-license information and delivery address.
  • Cart, product configuration, order, delivery, payment-status, cancellation and support records.
  • Security and diagnostic information such as request identifiers, authentication events and service errors.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate customers, operate the catalog and cart, process and deliver orders, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain business records and improve service reliability.

Service providers

NoboSole uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, and contracted infrastructure providers to host the storefront, API and database. These providers process information only to deliver their services to NoboSole. NoboSole does not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication sessions are stored in device-protected storage on Android. We keep profile and order data while an account is active and retain records longer only when needed for delivery, disputes, fraud prevention, security or legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You may sign out at any time and may request deletion of your account and associated data. Some transaction records may be retained when required by law; support will explain any such retention.
