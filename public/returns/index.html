Delivery, Returns & Refunds

Effective 28 July 2026

Order changes and cancellation

A pending order may be cancelled from the app when the cancellation action is available. Once processing or fulfilment begins, contact support immediately; cancellation may no longer be possible.

Check deliveries promptly

Confirm the package count and inspect products after delivery. Keep the invoice, packaging and clear photos when reporting missing, damaged or incorrect goods.

Return eligibility

Wholesale and made-to-order products may have product-specific return conditions. Support will review the order, product condition, reason for return and applicable sales agreement before authorizing a return. Do not send goods back without return instructions.

Refunds

Approved refunds are recorded against the original order and are completed using an agreed payment method. Processing time depends on the payment channel and any required inspection.
