Delete your NoboSole account

This request is available even if the app is no longer installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the support link below.
  2. Send the prefilled account-deletion message.
  3. Provide the Google-account email used for NoboSole when support asks for it.
  4. Complete identity verification. Never send your password or Google verification code.
Submit deletion request

What will be deleted

After verification, NoboSole will delete or anonymize the customer profile, saved addresses, active cart, authentication relationship and other personal data that is no longer required.

Records that may be retained

Order, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute and accounting records may be retained when required for legitimate business or legal obligations. Support will explain the expected completion time and any retained categories when confirming the request.

Alternative in-app path

In the NoboSole app, open Profile → Legal & account → Delete Account.
